# Team Lunch

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Interviewers:** The whole Tractorbeam team
**Scheduling:** Coordinate internally, then send candidate a calendar invite

## Interview Prep (Gem internal note)

The team gets a feel for what it's like to work alongside this person day-to-day, and the candidate gets an unfiltered look at Tractorbeam. This is a culture and collaboration signal, not a technical evaluation. Everyone at the lunch submits a scorecard.

## Timing Breakdown

| Time      | Section                                                                         |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-5 min   | Arrival, introductions, order food                                              |
| 5-20 min  | Casual conversation. Let it flow.                                               |
| 20-40 min | Candidate asks about the team, day-to-day work, culture                         |
| 40-55 min | Team members can ask about the candidate's interests, background, working style |
| 55-60 min | Wrap up, outline remaining steps                                                |

## Guidelines

- Be yourselves. This should feel like a normal team lunch, not a panel.
- No trick questions or technical grilling.
- Pay attention to: Do they ask good questions? Are they curious about the team? Do they listen well? Would you want to sit next to this person every day?
- Fill out your scorecard right after lunch while impressions are fresh.

## Suggested Conversation Starters (if things stall)

- "What's a project you've worked on that you enjoyed, and what made it fun?"
- "What does your ideal workday look like?"
- "What are you curious about with our team?"

## Scorecard

Use the default scorecard (Overall recommendation + Overall feedback). No custom questions.
