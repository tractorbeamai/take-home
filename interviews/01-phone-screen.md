# Phone Screen

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Interviewer:** Charlie or Wade
**Scheduling:** Request availability. Skip entirely for referrals.

## Interview Prep (Gem internal note)

This is a disqualification call, not an evaluation. The goal is to catch dealbreakers early and avoid wasting Charlie and Wade's time on candidates who can't work here. If none of the disqualifiers below apply, pass them through. Don't try to judge whether they're a great candidate on a 15-minute call.

**Disqualifiers (reject immediately):**

- Not in Chicago and not willing to relocate
- Needs visa sponsorship
- Gross experience mismatch (e.g., applying for a senior role with no relevant experience, or vice versa)
- Comp expectations wildly out of range
- Not available within a reasonable start window

If none of these apply, advance them.

## Timing Breakdown

| Time      | Section                                                                 |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-2 min   | Intro, set expectations for the call                                    |
| 2-8 min   | Logistics first: location, visa status, availability, comp expectations |
| 8-12 min  | Brief background and what drew them to Tractorbeam                      |
| 12-15 min | Candidate questions, next steps                                         |

## Suggested Questions

- "Are you based in Chicago, or open to relocating?"
- "Do you need visa sponsorship now or in the future?"
- "What's your availability? When could you start?"
- "What are your comp expectations?"
- "Give me the quick version: what are you working on now and why are you looking?"
