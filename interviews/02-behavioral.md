# Behavioral Interview

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Interviewer:** Charlie Hertz (Co-Founder)
**Scheduling:** Request availability

## Interview Prep (Gem internal note)

The first real conversation and the primary culture/motivation gate. Charlie is assessing whether this person will thrive in an early-stage consulting environment: ambiguity tolerance, ownership, client-facing presence. This is also a sell opportunity; candidates who meet Charlie should leave excited about Tractorbeam. Not everyone advances from here. If the fit isn't there, that's a fine outcome. Don't schedule the technical.

## Timing Breakdown

| Time | Section |
|------|---------|
| 0-3 min | Intro, warm up, set the tone (casual, conversational) |
| 3-12 min | Their story: what they've done, why they're looking |
| 12-20 min | Dig into consulting-relevant signals (ownership, ambiguity, stakeholders) |
| 20-25 min | Sell Tractorbeam: your story, the mission, what makes this different |
| 25-30 min | Their questions, next steps |

## Suggested Questions

### Their Story (3-12 min)
- "Tell me about the most impactful project you've worked on recently. What was your role, and what made it impactful?"
- "What's prompting you to look around right now? What would the ideal next step look like?"

### Consulting Signals (12-20 min)
- "Tell me about a time you had to figure something out with very little direction. How did you get started?"
- "Have you ever had to manage a difficult stakeholder or navigate competing priorities? What happened?"
- "What's something you shipped that you're proud of, where the hardest part wasn't the technical work?"

### Close / Sell (20-25 min)
- Share your own path to starting Tractorbeam. Be honest about what's hard and what's exciting.
- "What questions do you have about Tractorbeam or the role?"
