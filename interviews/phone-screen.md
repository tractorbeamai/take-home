# Phone Screen

**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Interviewer:** Charlie or Wade
**Scheduling:** Request availability. Skip entirely for referrals.

## Interview Prep (Gem internal note)

Quick filter for inbound/sourced candidates. We're checking basic communication, genuine interest in Tractorbeam, and whether the role is a realistic fit. This is a screen, not an evaluation — if there's any doubt, pass them through.

## Timing Breakdown

| Time | Section |
|------|---------|
| 0-2 min | Intro, set expectations for the call |
| 2-8 min | Candidate background and motivation |
| 8-12 min | Role overview and logistics (comp, location, start date) |
| 12-15 min | Candidate questions, next steps |

## Suggested Questions

- "Walk me through what you're working on right now and what's prompting you to look around."
- "What do you know about Tractorbeam, and what drew you to this role?"
- "What are your hard requirements — comp range, timing, location?"
- "Is there anything that would be a dealbreaker for you that we should talk about now?"
